Frequently Asked Questions

When are UK Lotto results announced?
UK National Lotto draws every Wednesday and Saturday at approximately 7:45 PM GMT.
What days does Powerball draw?
Powerball draws every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at 10:59 PM EST.
How many times does UK49s draw per day?
UK49s draws twice daily — Lunchtime at 12:49 PM GMT and Teatime at 5:49 PM GMT, seven days a week.
Can I buy tickets on this website?
No. This is a results information website only. We do not sell tickets.
How often are results updated?
Results are fetched automatically from official government sources after each draw.
